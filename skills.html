THE SKILL SIGNAL

Tools matter. Judgment connects them.

Pharmaceutical analysis combines technical tools with knowledge of the underlying data. Explore the tools, vendors and datasets mentioned in US postings across commercial, clinical, scientific and operational work.

skillData = FileAttachment("outputs/us-employer-inventory/web/skills-data.json").json()
scope = skillData.scopes[scopeKey]
skillRows = scope.skills.map(d => ({...d, share: d.denominator ? 100 * d.records / d.denominator : 0})).filter(d => d.name.toLowerCase().includes(skillSearch.trim().toLowerCase())).sort((a, b) => b[skillMetric] - a[skillMetric] || a.name.localeCompare(b.name))
selectedSkill = skillRows.find(d => d.name === skillName)
evidenceRows = selectedSkill ? selectedSkill.sources.filter(d => evidenceEmployer === "All employers" || d.company === evidenceEmployer) : []

The blue-white-pink identity is coherent and the restored circular molecular hero suits the pharma/analytics subject. Preserve it. Its biggest remaining problem is explaining multiple evidence populations before asking the visitor to interpret their numbers.

## Strengths
- Primary Jobs and training-prospect actions have clear visual priority and become comfortably stacked on mobile.
- The historical-data and proposed-certificate caveats sit beside the main actions, reducing false expectations about live vacancies or measured graduate outcomes.
- Broad training evidence explicitly distinguishes employer labels and review candidates; sources and methods remain easy to reach.

## Priorities
1. **P2: Separate evidence populations at the decision point.** The homepage introduces 1,353 all-geography historical records, 15 selected employers / 9,493 jobs, and then 5,822 analytics candidates across 1,254 employer labels. Labels are accurate but reconciling them remains the visitor's task. Put concise scope captions directly under the two primary routes: selected-employer job explorer versus broad employer-training screen. Keep their respective counts together. Suggested command: clarify. Success check: a new visitor can explain why the two employer totals differ without opening Methods.
2. **P2: Add one-step recovery for Jobs filters.** Jobs has four selectors and search, but no clear-all action; the empty state explains missing evidence without helping reverse the filter combination. Add Reset filters beside the result count and in the empty state. Suggested command: harden. Success check: any zero-result combination can return to the default inventory in one action. Preserve the all-employer default.
3. **P2: Reduce mobile metric-label wrapping.** The selected-employer heading and extraction timestamp wrap into several short lines inside the two-column metrics region. The comparison becomes tall and the broader-training evidence moves far below the primary action. Shorten the visual metric label and put the timestamp in a shared evidence note, while retaining complete provenance in Methods. Suggested command: adapt/typeset. Success check: at 390px the four metric labels are readable as short labels and dates do not dominate the row.

## Cognitive load and emotional journey
The hero has two decisive actions, which is effective. Jobs presents five simultaneous controls, and the homepage metric strip mixes collection volume, employer count, industry growth and course count: complementary facts with different denominators rather than directly comparable KPIs. The molecular hero creates interest; the evidence caveat reassures; the mixed totals are the main comprehension valley. The footer maintains source accountability without an aggressive conversion pitch.

# Certificate website format, November 8, 2026

The approved design direction is a standalone non-credit certificate, self-paced online within semester offerings, with four sequential courses paced at 3 + 4 + 4 + 3 weeks. Source: PCDC v2 syllabus draft.md in the Pharma Certificate project, updated November 7, 2026, and the user decisions in this chat.

## Brochure comparison

Reviewed both user-provided PDFs: bu-paralegal-brochure 2026.pdf (four pages) and Paralegal-flyer 2026.pdf (two pages). Their presentation makes duration, delivery, course content, practical skills, and enrollment information easy to locate. Applied that information hierarchy to the existing Certificate page while preserving the website design.

The brochure describes seven two-week modules with module closure and deadlines. The flyer describes no scheduled lectures and optional office hours. Neither establishes unrestricted self-paced study. The pharma design therefore follows the user's self-paced requirement, with suggested weeks and a semester completion window.

Do not transfer the paralegal program's $3,995 tuition, six yearly certificate start dates, three yearly standalone-module starts, faculty, contact information, legal database access, or digital badge promises into the pharma program. No paralegal PDFs are republished as pharma program materials.

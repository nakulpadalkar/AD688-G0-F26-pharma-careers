How many companies, and how large?

2,305 employer firms operated 2,882 pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing establishments, employing 314,242 people in those establishments in 2022. This is the Census SUSB NAICS 3254 population, not the full biopharma ecosystem or a list of qualified certificate buyers.

Broader biopharma context

PhRMA’s summary of the newer TEConomy report describes nearly 1.1 million direct biopharma jobs in 2024 across the states, DC and Puerto Rico. California, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, North Carolina, Pennsylvania and Texas each exceed 50,000 direct jobs. This broader industry definition includes more than manufacturing; it is not comparable to the narrower Census count, and the figures must not be added. PhRMA / TEConomy summary.

R&D-only biotechs, analytics vendors, CROs and consultancies may also buy training. The manufacturing firm count does not enumerate them. A deduplicated national count of commercially relevant buyers and eligible employees is not yet established.

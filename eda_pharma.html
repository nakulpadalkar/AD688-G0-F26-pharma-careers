Evidence & methods

Sources, geographic coverage and comparability limits for pharmaceutical analytics career evidence.

Pharmaceutical career evidence answers three different questions: what employers describe in job advertisements, where the collected roles are located, and how related occupations are projected to change. Employer postings, METEOR and BLS address these questions at different scales. The source review is dated November 2, 2026.

The saved IndustryClassification audit screened 7,526,775 local employability posting records dated January 1, 2025 through September 30, 2026 (UTC). The population includes all geographies and active/inactive history. The figures describe a saved historical collection.

BLS industry table 2.11 reports pharmaceutical and medicine manufacturing (matrix code 325400): 355.4 thousand jobs in 2025, 380.5 thousand projected in 2035, 7.1% total growth and 0.7% compound annual growth. US employer inventory and freshness

The US inventory draws on 18,443 local PostgreSQL records and 4,549 Azure records associated with 15 employers. It includes all available dates, including records without a reported posting date. The source population is the stored job-posting collection; coverage varies by employer and collection period.

The employer scope includes AbbVie, Iovance Biotherapeutics, Amgen, AstraZeneca, Takeda, Gilead Sciences, Regeneron, Pfizer, Novartis, Sanofi, Neurocrine Biosciences, Otsuka, Teva, Vertex Pharmaceuticals and IQVIA. IQVIA is included as a data/analytics employer as well as a vendor mentioned in other employers’ descriptions.

Matching source/external identities and normalized requisition URLs join records across and within stores. For Workday URLs, the employer and requisition ID are used across locale and URL variants. Local text is preferred within a matched group. No group is created from a matching title alone. Re-advertisements with different source identities may therefore remain separate. Counts describe combined posting identities, not independently confirmed unique vacancies.

Relevance and geography

Roles qualify through data, analytics, informatics, biostatistics, epidemiology, HEOR, forecasting or business/operations analyst titles; through commercial-data titles and duties; or through explicit analytical duties paired with a technical tool such as SQL, Python, R, SAS, Tableau or Power BI. This broader inventory includes clinical, scientific and operational data careers. Generic employer boilerplate, Excel alone, and non-analytical laboratory or administrative roles do not qualify. The rules identify relevant records from titles and descriptions; individual matches have not all been manually reviewed. The certificate focuses on commercial data; inclusion in the broader inventory does not imply that the certificate prepares a learner for every listed specialty.

US evidence comes from an explicit country, a recognizable US location, or the location segment of an employer recruiting URL. A reference to supporting US teams in the job title or description is insufficient. Contradictory country evidence and unresolved locations are excluded and counted. Regeneron’s Tarrytown and Sleepy Hollow recruiting locations are mapped only for that employer using its official US location directory. Other city-only locations are not automatically geocoded. Employer-name collisions such as unrelated Vertex firms and Gilead Community Services are excluded.

The inventory describes historical advertisements whose current availability has not been verified. Earlier manually reviewed employer pages form a separate archive. A zero employer count means no records met the relevance and US-location criteria in this collection.

Posting dates and collection-date proxies

The raw local extract contains 6,654 source-reported posting timestamps and 11,789 records without them. Azure has 2,360 source timestamps and 2,189 missing. Every missing-date record in these snapshots has a database creation timestamp. Where a posting date is absent, the explorer uses the earliest creation timestamp in the combined record as First recorded (date proxy). This is an approximate collection date, not a recovered or imputed posting date. Migration or batch ingestion can make it much later than the real posting.

The complete source metadata contains all 22,992 source rows, including overlaps and excluded records. Posting dates and first-recorded dates remain separate. The US inventory contains the records that meet the employer, location and relevance criteria after matching duplicate identities.

Independent filters and skills

Career level, data product/dataset, database store and text search filter the full US inventory independently of employer selection. The headline and per-employer table show the selected combination. Employer counts reflect the same combination of filters as the displayed postings.

IQVIA, Symphony, MMIT, claims and patient-level data are discovery signals. IQVIA is also an employer, so generic IQVIA company-introduction wording is excluded from its skill signal. Claims related to employee benefits are not treated as commercial claims-data skills. Each detected skill counts once per posting identity. Mentions do not establish that a named vendor is required, that all listed alternatives are mandatory, or that the candidate needs access to a licensed dataset.

The skills explorer offers the full eligible US inventory and the subset mentioning tracked data products/datasets. These percentages describe the collected and screened records; they are not industry-wide prevalence estimates. The historical METEOR map remains a separate older snapshot. BLS remains independent market context.

The inventory spans pharmaceutical data careers beyond commercial analytics. Clinical, scientific and operational specialties can require additional training beyond the proposed certificate.

Experience expressions can refer to total experience, a specialty, management work or a degree alternative. The analysis retains these expressions rather than treating them as a single minimum. Degree mentions combine required, preferred and alternative credentials; equivalent-experience language therefore requires interpretation in the context of the full posting.

The observed duties connect pharmaceutical source knowledge to data integration, quality checks and interpretation. This supports the curriculum’s emphasis on those relationships, while leaving the effectiveness of the proposed training to future evaluation. Vendor names and dataset types are distinct: IQVIA LAAD integrates patient-level claims, while MMIT’s documentation describes drug formulary coverage by health plan. The proposed curriculum can teach these concepts with synthetic or authorized data.

Reproducibility and downloads

  • Aggregate METEOR evidence: counts, geography, monthly records, skills, limitations and source SHA-256 hashes.
  • Official benchmarks: values, units, vintages and primary-source URLs.
  • Combined US posting inventory: employer, role, US evidence, provenance, detected qualifications and source URLs; no full descriptions.
  • Coverage and screening summary: source counts, employer counts, exclusions and qualification observations.
  • Skills analysis: scope denominators and source-linked mention counts.
  • Earlier direct-page research archive: the original ten manually reviewed examples, separate from database inventory counts.
  • Historical annotated examples: thirteen older METEOR examples.

The downloads preserve source dates, definitions and analysis denominators. Employer advertisements describe hiring expectations; evaluation of certificate outcomes would require separate graduate follow-up data.

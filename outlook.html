Growth, with the right context.

Compare national occupations that overlap with commercial analytics work. These benchmarks include all industries and experience levels; they are not expected certificate starting salaries.

benchmarks = FileAttachment("outputs/us-employer-inventory/web/outlook-data.json").json()
occupations = benchmarks.occupations
metricLabels = new Map([["Projected growth (%)", "growth"], ["Median annual wage ($)", "median"], ["Annual projected openings", "openings"]])
formatMetric = (value, key) => key === "median" ? "$" + value.toLocaleString("en-US") : key === "growth" ? value + "%" : value.toLocaleString("en-US")
comparison = occupations.filter(d => selectedOccupations.includes(d.name)).sort((a, b) => b[metric] - a[metric])

How METEOR compares

BLS estimates employment and projected growth. METEOR observes advertisements in a changing collection. O*NET describes work. Their different populations provide complementary views of careers, rather than a common vacancy-rate measure.
